Two Cities

Your Journey.

Yinchuan and Zhongwei — two representative cities of China's innovation in the west.

Yinchuan.

Ningxia's capital — home to the Helan Mountain wine region, the Ningdong energy base, and monocrystalline-silicon / PV materials. Once the capital of the Western Xia dynasty.

Industry focusGreen energy · New materials · Smart manufacturing · Wine

Zhongwei.

A national node of China's 'East-Data West-Compute' project — a major data-centre / computing cluster — and the home of Zhongning goji berries and the Shapotou desert-meets-Yellow-River wonder.

Industry focusComputing power · Data centres · Goji
Site Visits

Where China's West Powers Up.

Ningxia's representative industries — and where overseas technology and innovation are genuinely complementary.

dns
1Western Cloud BaseZhongwei

A national node of China's 'East-Data West-Compute' project, Zhongwei hosts one of the country's largest data-centre and computing clusters. A cool climate and abundant local green power make it a low-cost, low-carbon home for large-scale compute, and global cloud players have already built here. It is a natural landing point for AI, cloud and data-driven applications from overseas.

Computing powerData centres
bolt
2Baofeng Green HydrogenNingdong

Baofeng runs one of the world's largest solar-powered green-hydrogen demonstration projects, producing hydrogen at industrial scale directly from photovoltaic electricity. It sits within the Ningdong energy base, one of China's most concentrated clean- and coal-chemical energy zones. For overseas players in hydrogen, storage and green manufacturing, it offers a rare full-scale, real-world testbed.

Green hydrogenNew energy
solar_power
3Longi Silicon BaseYinchuan

A major monocrystalline-silicon and PV-materials manufacturing base of a global solar leader, sitting at the very upstream of the world's solar supply chain. Ningxia's low-cost green power underpins its competitiveness in high-purity silicon. It is a strong counterpart for overseas semiconductor, PV and advanced-materials innovation.

PV materialsMonocrystalline silicon
precision_manufacturing
4Kocel Smart CastingYinchuan

Kocel is a global pioneer in smart foundries and industrial 3D printing, replacing traditional moulds with large-scale 3D-printed sand casting. Its 'lights-out' digital factories in Yinchuan turn out complex castings for energy, aerospace and heavy equipment. It is a natural partner for overseas advanced-manufacturing, additive-manufacturing and industrial-AI innovation.

Smart manufacturingIndustrial 3D printing
wine_bar
5Helan Mountain WineryYinchuan

The eastern foothills of the Helan Mountains are China's premier wine region, home to award-winning estates recognised on the world stage. High-value specialty agriculture here blends terroir, brand and tourism. It opens complementary opportunities in agritech, food tech, branding, export and cultural tourism for overseas partners.

WineSpecialty agriculture
eco
6Zhongning GojiZhongwei

Zhongning is the acknowledged home of Chinese goji berries, with leading producers moving from raw agriculture into functional foods and bio-extraction. Modern processing, standardisation and health-focused products are reshaping a traditional crop. It offers overseas biotech, nutraceutical and food-processing players a distinctive, high-value agricultural base.

Functional foodGoji

Culture & Heritage

Where High-Tech Meets Ancient Heritage.

Ningxia's unique heritage — where mysterious ancient history meets the Yellow River and the desert.

Yinchuan

Western Xia Imperial Tombs

The 'Oriental Pyramids' at the foot of the Helan Mountains — the mysterious relics of the lost Tangut / Western Xia civilisation, a landscape found nowhere else in China.

Zhongwei

Shapotou

Where the Yellow River meets the Tengger Desert — a spectacular landscape and a world-renowned feat of sand control, blending culture, nature and human ingenuity.

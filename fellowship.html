Who Can Apply

For Students Aiming to Study Abroad.

The fellowship is built for high school and university students in China who plan to pursue their next degree overseas — undergraduate or graduate, outside China. Admission is selective and cohorts are kept small, so we only accept students we can genuinely place and mentor.

Who Should Apply

High school & university students planning to study overseas

  • check_circleHigh schoolers aiming for overseas undergraduate study, and undergraduates targeting master's or PhD programmes abroad
  • check_circleSet on pursuing a higher degree outside China
  • check_circleCurious about technology and entrepreneurship — no coding required
  • check_circleAble to work in English and commit time during school holidays

Small Cohorts. Limited Places. Apply Early.

Admission is selective and each cohort is intentionally small. Tell us about your student — or yourself — and we will arrange an introductory conversation.

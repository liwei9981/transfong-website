- **Our History:** Founded in 2018 in Singapore by former members of the technology transfer and commercialization team of the Agency for Science, Technology and Research (A*STAR), Singapore. We entered the Chinese market at the end of 2019. Our global headquarters is located at One North, Singapore.
    
- **Founding Team:**
    
    - Dr. Li Wei – Co-founder & Chairman: Dr. Li Wei is dedicated to fostering the cross-border growth of tech companies. With nearly 20 years of experience in technology transfer and commercialization, including over a decade at Singapore's A*STAR, he is skilled in investment, incubation, IP management, and international collaboration. He holds a Ph.D. and Bachelor's in Microelectronics from Nanyang Technological University, and an MBA from INSEAD.
        
    - **Tyler Huang – Co-Founder & CEO:** Tyler Huang has nearly 20 years of experience in high-tech enterprise and project management, including over 10 years at NXP Semiconductors. As a Lean Six Sigma certified project manager, he is well-versed in international technology collaboration. He holds dual Bachelor's degrees in Electronic Engineering and English from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, and a Master's in Technology Management from the National University of Singapore.

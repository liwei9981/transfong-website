Culture & Heritage

Where High-Tech Meets Ancient Heritage.

Experiences that connect innovation with the depth of Chinese culture and the flavours of the autumn season.

Wuxi

Wuxi Hairy Crabs

Primarily sourced from the pristine waters of Taihu Lake and Jiangyin, Wuxi's hairy crabs are a celebrated autumn delicacy — the perfect seasonal reward as our journey lands at the height of crab season.

Nanjing

Confucius Temple

Experience the poetic nightfall with 1,700 years of Qinhuai Heritage. A timeless cultural landmark where history, philosophy, and vibrant night markets converge along the ancient river.
